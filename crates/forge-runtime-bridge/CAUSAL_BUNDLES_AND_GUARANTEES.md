# Causal Bundles And Guarantees

This guide explains the bridge's trust claims at a higher level.

The bridge should be able to preserve one coherent causal story across:

- truth trigger
- routing
- truth-view evaluation
- speculation
- writeback or promotion
- replay

## What A Guarantee Means Here

A guarantee is not a slogan.

It should be recoverable from canonical bridge artifacts and typed outcomes.

Important bridge guarantees include:

- deterministic routing for the same truth input
- explicit truth-view basis
- branch-local speculative isolation
- discard as a positive zero-residue story
- promotion as an explicit authority crossing
- replay that preserves bridge meaning, not only final output

## Why Bundles Matter

One isolated record is usually not enough to explain a real cross-runtime
story.

The bridge needs bundle-shaped evidence that preserves:

- what changed
- what was selected
- what was evaluated
- what remained local
- what crossed into authority

That is how diagnosis and certification stay offline-auditable instead of
depending on host memory.

## Where This Shows Up Publicly

This layer sits mostly in specialist retained records, replay bundles, and
diagnostics explanations.

It is the conceptual bridge between:

- "I used the runtime"

and:

- "I can prove what the runtime meant"

## Product Rule

If the bridge still needs host logs or informal operator memory to explain a
causal result, the guarantee surface is incomplete.
