## Scope And Source State

This ledger audits C.6 Phase 10, **Hostile Courtroom, Mutation Closure, And
Successor Handoff**, against the complete C.6 engineering specification and
the inherited C.5.1 guarantees that Phase 10 exercises.

The audit began from Phase 8 commit
`659c5775de9637a2f8a7c1c7d7205d0851ada683` on branch `worth-store`.
Phase 9 documentation changes remain a separate completed, uncommitted scope.
Phase 10 is uncommitted and remains open until every guarantee below is
`PROVED` or has a justified `N/A`.

## Adversarial Constraint

> A fresh serving process opens a real Store containing 114,486,784 logical
> payload bytes with 65,536 resident bytes and a 6,979,584-byte total live
> envelope. It must survive exact pin, duplicate-fault, eviction, copy,
> dirty/writeback, speculative, scope, cancellation, stale-generation, and
> shutdown pressure without a whole-record or whole-Store materialization,
> competing work runtime, counterfeit authority, or unclassified effect.
> Independent fresh processes must produce, serve, verify, and reopen the same
> persisted truth. Every controlled defect must fail its nearest causal
> predicate. The canonical staged schedule is not the whole concurrency proof:
> 16 revision-derived schedule seeds must perturb every lawful harness
> decision family and remain exactly replayable.

## Allocation Observer Closure Contract

`W06`, `A01`, and `F06` close only when the certification observer retains the
ordered allocation-boundary events rather than deriving attribution from the
19 aggregate cells. Each retained event must carry:

- the exact residency dimension and operation scope, with absence explicit for
  genuinely unscoped lifecycle allocation;
- requested and actual units without rewriting an early reservation as a later
  materialization;
- the operating-system process identity captured at the boundary;
- the real Store physical-operation sequence when that identity already exists,
  and explicit absence when metadata admission, candidate construction, dirty
  replacement, or pre-work bounded admission precedes identity minting; and
- an event kind that distinguishes admission, release, denial, allocator
  failure, and later actualization.

For metadata, resident, and dirty-replacement bytes, actualization must consume
a matching earlier admission from the same dimension and scope. Metadata
matches the concrete charged capacity; resident and dirty replacement match the
requested reservation because actual capacity may be lower. Aggregate equality
does not legalize a trace that admits after materialization.

The trace is certification evidence only. It opens no pool or Store authority,
does not import Signal, `worth-proof`, Foundational, or Store runtime types into
the buffer pool, and cannot affect an admission outcome. The Store may translate
an already-minted `PhysicalWorkIdentity` into the buffer pool's observational
operation sequence; it may not invent one retroactively. Aggregate counters
remain the independent reconciliation target, not the source of the raw trace.

Closure proof must reject at least these nearest-predicate defects while all
unrelated totals remain valid: wrong scope, requested/actual transposition,
wrong process, wrong operation sequence, removal of one materialization event,
and materialization reordered before matching admission. The final evidence
bundle must retain enough raw fields for the runner to perform those checks
without trusting stdout prose.
